HAZEL'S BUG ADVENTURE v2 (iPhone web app)
1. Upload index.html (+ optional butterfly.png) to a GitHub repo, turn on GitHub Pages (https needed for camera/location/mic).
2. Open the Pages link in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen. Allow Camera, Location, Microphone.
CAMERA FLOW: photo -> Look closer (opens Google Lens; pick the photo, come back, tap "I'm back") -> type/say the name (bug name suggestions appear from iNaturalist and fill the box when tapped) -> Save. Facts come from Wikipedia when a suggestion is tapped.
VOICES: tap the 🎙️ button (top left) to switch British lady / Deep storyteller / Country sweetheart. Voices depend on what is installed on the iPhone (Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices: download "Enhanced" voices such as Kate, Daniel, Samantha for best sound).
STORIES: book has a cover with the date range of bugs found. "Grown-ups edit" lets a parent change any page; Share > Review & PDF edits then prints (Print > Save as PDF).
REAL BUTTERFLY: put a top-view zebra swallowtail PNG with transparent background named butterfly.png next to index.html and it is used for the launch screen.
IDLE: butterfly asks "Hey butter beans!" after 2 minutes of no touching. Returning from Google Maps/Earth/Lens gives a "Welcome back".
NOT POSSIBLE: Google gives no way to auto-read Lens results; Morgan Freeman or singer voices cannot be copied; Google pages cannot show our back arrow (use Safari's Done/Back or close the tab).
